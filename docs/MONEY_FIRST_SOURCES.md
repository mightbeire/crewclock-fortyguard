## FortyGuard evidence boundary

No source above changes the hard limit imposed by the local cache:

- usable cached thermal maps exist for Phoenix, Las Vegas, Los Angeles, and Atlanta;
- Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, New York, and Portland cached geography probes returned zero heatmap cells and must be treated as coverage failures, not invented maps;
- no cached cold-event FortyGuard analysis exists for Texas gas or Midwest winter roads;
- surface/tile temperature is not transformer winding temperature, WBGT, rail temperature, concrete mix temperature, product temperature, or certified aviation weather;
- FortyGuard is therefore an investigation/screening input. The operational workflow must retain telemetry, approved equations, policies, qualified personnel, and human authorization.
