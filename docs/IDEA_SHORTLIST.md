# Idea shortlist: 15 → 6

## Survivors

1. Pavement Window Agent — strongest industrial wedge; combines worker scheduling and asset-quality review without pretending to automate engineering.
2. Thermal Sequence Planner — clear agent loop, high recurrence, excellent 20-second before/after.
3. DockShift Orchestrator — specific enterprise workflow and low public duplication.
4. Data Center Cooling Readiness Agent — high-value facilities problem, but telemetry dependency is real.
5. Cooling Center Logistics Agent — high impact and strong agentic allocation, but public-sector data/deployment risk.
6. Transit Stop Intervention Triage — measurable and FortyGuard-central, but existing heat-planning tools raise duplication risk.

## Why the others died

- Construction Work/Rest Planner: rejected as a standalone product because worker heat alerts and break schedules are already obvious; only survives when narrowed to an industrial decision such as pavement/pour windows.
- Event Cooling Station Planner: useful, but event logistics and station placement are less recurring/defensible than the finalists and require crowd/access data.
- Utility Outage Crew Stager: viable but too close to the field-service sequence concept; keep as a reusable workflow variant.
- Commercial Property Retrofit Triage: the vendored quickstart and existing products already cover much of the workflow; not enough new agent wedge.
- Airport Ramp Rotation Planner: compelling but depends on airport-specific operational data and heightened safety validation.
- Concrete Pour Window Agent: merged into the Pavement Window Agent family because the stronger wedge is thermal work-window selection with human engineering approval.
- Manufacturing Yard Task Allocator: credible but less distinctive than DockShift and not as easy to communicate.
- Delivery Hot-Zone Router: rejected for duplication risk 4; heat-aware routing is an obvious idea with public research and product precedents.
- Harvest Window Assistant: rejected for geographic/data uncertainty and weaker account fit.
- Heat-Aware Inspection Queue: reusable infrastructure pattern, but too generic without selecting one asset class.
